Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup — Species Aptitudes Guide

Aptitudes determine how quickly each species learns skills. A positive aptitude
means the species learns that skill faster than average; a negative aptitude
means slower. An aptitude of 0 is average learning speed.

Skills Available in Crawl:

Combat Skills:
  Fighting — General combat proficiency, affects all attack types.
  Short Blades — Daggers, short swords, and similar weapons.
  Long Blades — Swords, great swords, and longswords.
  Axes — Axes and greataxes.
  Maces and Flails — Maces, clubs, flails, and morning stars.
  Polearms — Spears, halberds, and similar long weapons.
  Staves — Quarterstaves and magical staves.
  Ranged Weapons — Bows, crossbows, and slings.
  Throwing — Thrown weapons like javelins and grenades.
  Unarmed Combat — Fighting with bare hands and tentacles.

Defence Skills:
  Armour — Reduces damage taken when wearing armour.
  Dodging — Chance to avoid being hit entirely.
  Shields — Using shields to block incoming attacks.
  Stealth — Moving without being detected by monsters.

Magic Skills:
  Spellcasting — General spellcasting ability (affects mana and cast chance).
  Conjurations — Spells that create destructive energy (fireballs, etc).
  Hexes — Spells that debilitate and control enemies.
  Summoning — Spells that call allies to fight for you.
  Necromancy — Spells involving death and undead.
  Translocations — Spells for movement (teleport, blink, etc).
  Fire Magic — Spells using fire and heat.
  Ice Magic — Spells using cold and ice.
  Air Magic — Spells using wind and lightning.
  Earth Magic — Spells using earth and stone.
  Alchemy — Spells involving transmutation and chemistry.
  Forgecraft — Spells for creating and enhancing equipment.

Other Skills:
  Invocations — Using divine abilities granted by your god.
  Evocations — Using magical items and wands.
  Shapeshifting — Transforming into different forms.

Species Notes:
  Humans have average aptitudes across all skills but gain experience 15% faster.
  Elves excel at magic but struggle with physical combat.
  Dwarves are sturdy fighters but poor at magic.
  Octopuses have unique tentacle-based combat and excellent swimming.
  Nagas have natural poison resistance and good stealth.
  Trolls have incredible regeneration and brute strength.
  Formicids are natural diggers with unique foraging abilities.

Hepliaklqana's Ancestor:
  The Ancestor companion of Hepliaklqana adapts its form based on the player's
  progress. It can become a Knight (melee specialist), Battlemage (combat caster),
  or Hexer (crowd control specialist). The Ancestor's capabilities scale with
  the player's own skill development and experience level.
